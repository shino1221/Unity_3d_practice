# Cenji Low Poly Fantasy Crystal Pack

## 57 FREE GAME-READY MODELS

A collection of **57 stylized low-poly 3D models** designed for indie game developers, game jams, RPGs, dungeon crawlers, VR experiences, and fantasy environments.

### Included in this Free Download:

- **20 Crystal Formations**: Pre-composed crystal clusters, spires, geodes, and arches on bedrock anchors (`PROP_01` to `PROP_20`).
- **12 Standalone Gems**: Individual crystal points, diamonds, spikes, and shards for weapon sockets, loot pickups, item drops, and custom scattering (`GEM_01` to `GEM_12`).
- **25 Modular Environment Rocks**: Large boulders, plinths, flat walkable platforms, tall pillars/spires, and rubble clusters across 5 distinct biomes (Slate, Volcanic Basalt, Glacial Frost, Corrupted Abyssal, Ancient Masonry) (`ROCK_` files).

### Formats Included:
- **FBX** (Clean exports with embedded materials, scale applied, pivot at base $Z=0.000$)
- **GLB / glTF 2.0** (Materials pre-assigned, ready for web, Godot, Blender, Unity, and Unreal)

### Supported Engines & Workflows:
- Unity (URP / HDRP / Built-in)
- Unreal Engine 5
- Godot 4
- Blender
- VR / AR Projects
- Web / Three.js / Babylon.js / PlayCanvas

### Model Specifications:
- **Stylized low-poly geometry**: Highly optimized polycounts (9 to 408 polygons, average ~85 polygons per model).
- **Materials**: Color palette Principled BSDF materials assigned to all meshes where supported.
- **Sensible Origins & Transforms**: Every asset has its origin precisely placed at the bottom ground contact point ($Z=0.000$). Rotation is $(0,0,0)$ and scale is $(1,1,1)$.
- **No Colliders Included**: No colliders are included in the raw exports. Users should add collision components appropriate to their game engine and gameplay needs (box, capsule, convex hull, or simplified mesh collider).
- **Attribution**: Attribution is appreciated, but not required.
